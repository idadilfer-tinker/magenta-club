The problem

Enterprise design and startup design are not the same job.

Most designers — and most people hiring them — only know one of these two worlds.

Inside a large company

  • A design system already exists
  • Dozens of designers to lean on
  • Process, precedent, and support built in
  • Someone else owns the roadmap

Inside your portfolio company

  • Nothing exists yet — it gets built from zero
  • One hire owns brand, product and speed at once
  • Ambiguity is the whole job
  • Every decision compounds fast, for better or worse
Most design hires are trained for the left column. Your companies need the right one.
The cost

A design mis-hire doesn't cost a salary. It costs a fundraising cycle.

Every month spent onboarding the wrong designer — or managing one without a design background who can't tell the difference — is a month retention, activation and product-market-fit signals don't move. Those are the numbers the next round gets priced on.

Our edge

Three things we do differently.

Not theory — the specific mechanics that make design work for a startup's speed, not against it.

01

We don't place just a designer in your team. We place a design strategy in your product DNA.

Every engagement starts with our full senior team in the room — not one junior generalist hoping to figure it out. From there, we embed the single product designer best matched to your stage for the first stretch of the build (the shape differs for a seed-stage startup versus a scaling team).

Not any senior designer will do. Not a junior one either. Startup design is its own craft, and we've built a team fluent in it.

02

We build the work — then we build what keeps building it.

We ship the core product and brand design. Before we leave, we train AI agents on your product strategy and design DNA — so your team keeps producing on-brand work long after the engagement ends.

You don't re-buy design from scratch every quarter.

03

We are onboarding and retention specialists.

We own growth design across the customer lifecycle — shaping acquisition, activation, engagement, monetization, and retention as one system, not five disconnected hires.

We built our own tool to find and interview true early adopters the right way, so onboarding and education are designed to make the product stick — and you get the user insight that predicts retention before the metrics do.
